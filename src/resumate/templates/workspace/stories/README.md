# Stories

Each file here is one work story: a specific moment that shows how you think and what
you care about. Agents draw on these for cover letters (and you can reuse them for
interview prep). The resume says *what* you did; stories say *how* and *why*.

Write them in your own voice — rough is fine. Good stories are specific: names of
systems, real numbers, what was hard, what you'd do differently. One file per story:

```markdown
---
title: Short memorable title
when: 2023
where: Company or context
tags: [leadership, reliability, ambiguity]   # themes the story demonstrates
---

## Situation
## What I did
## Result
## Why it matters to me
```

The "why it matters to me" section is the most valuable part for cover letters:
it's what connects your experience to a company's mission in a way that sounds like you.
