## Situation
Our team averaged 35 pages a week, most of them non-actionable. Two people had
cited on-call in their exit interviews.

## What I did
Volunteered to own the problem. Categorized three months of pages, deleted or
downgraded alerts nobody acted on, defined SLOs for our four critical services, and
wrote runbooks for the top ten remaining alerts. Made "fix the alert" an accepted
outcome of any incident review.

## Result
Pages dropped about 60% within a quarter, and the next engagement survey showed
the team's on-call satisfaction jump from the bottom quartile to above average.

## Why it matters to me
Sustainable teams build better systems. I care about the unglamorous work that keeps
good engineers from burning out.
